01 · Method

How a change reaches your store.

Safe at every step, and nothing lands without your yes.

A front panel of three modules: me, staging and your store, joined by patch cables. Your store sends an ask; my agents build it on a copy of your store in staging, where a failed check stays; when every check passes, your store gets a ready flag; you send it back once, then accept; I press the V key, the change goes live, and your store's cards change.

02 · The system

Agents . Checks . I .

Nothing reaches your store until it is proved on a copy, and nothing ships without my yes.

One strip: an agent writes the change on a copy, line by line; four checks go blue one after the other while the readout counts them to four of four; then I press my V key and the readout says signed, ready to ship.
